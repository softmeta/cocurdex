# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for
everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing confidential information.
- Help keep discussions relevant and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- sustained disruption, intimidation, or bad-faith participation;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
project and its participants.

Report conduct concerns privately through the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and prefix the report title with `Conduct:`. Do not
include sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Maintainers will limit
access to the report and consider context, severity, and patterns of behavior
when deciding a response.
